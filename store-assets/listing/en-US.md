## Full description

TWINO is a modern English-learning app built for Kurdish speakers. Learn at your pace with structured lessons, practical examples, speaking activities, and motivating daily progress.

LEARN WITH A CLEAR PATH

Follow guided units that build vocabulary, listening, reading, grammar, and everyday communication step by step.

PRACTICE REAL ENGLISH

Use role-play, pronunciation activities, reading practice, slang, and conversation exercises inspired by real situations.

STAY MOTIVATED

Track XP, streaks, daily goals, quests, and lesson progress. TWINO keeps the next useful step clear without making learning feel heavy.

MADE FOR KURDISH SPEAKERS

Choose Kurdish-friendly guidance and fonts while learning natural English. Core lessons work without an account; sign in when you want progress sync and protected cloud AI features.

FREE FIRST RELEASE

This release is free. Paid subscriptions and external checkout are not enabled.

Some voice and AI features require microphone access, an internet connection, and a signed-in account. You can keep using non-voice core lessons without microphone access.
