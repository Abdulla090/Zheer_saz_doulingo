TWINO ENGLISH

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 17, 2026

Overview

TWINO helps Kurdish speakers learn English. The first public release is free. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Data on your device

The app stores learning progress, XP, streak, language, Kurdish font choice, AI practice history, and settings on your device. Native authentication tokens use protected device credential storage.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address, profile, preferences, avatar, and learning progress so they can sync across sessions. Core lessons remain available without an account.

Microphone and cloud AI

Speaking practice uses the microphone only after permission is granted. When you choose a Gemini-powered feature, the recorded audio, requested phrase, typed answer, transcript, and related prompt may be sent to Google Gemini to return learning feedback. Cloud AI requires an internet connection and a signed-in account.

Your choices

You can deny microphone access and continue with non-voice lessons. Permanently delete your TWINO account and synced data from Settings → Your Account → Delete Account. Clear remaining local data through system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

TWINO's first public release is intended for learners aged 13 and older. A parent or guardian should supervise any younger learner and decide whether they may create an account or use microphone and cloud AI features.

Contact

For privacy questions or a deletion request when the in-app option is unavailable, email support@twino.app from the account email address.
