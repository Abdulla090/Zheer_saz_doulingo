# Twino English — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 17, 2026

Twino English helps Kurdish speakers learn English. This document mirrors the in-app Privacy Policy (Settings → Privacy Policy).

## Summary

- **Free app** — paid subscriptions and external checkout are disabled in this release.
- **On-device data** — progress, XP, streak, language, font, AI history, and settings are stored on your device.
- **Optional account sync** — Supabase stores account, profile, preferences, avatar choice, and learning progress for signed-in users.
- **Optional profile photo** — a signed-in user's chosen photo is uploaded to Supabase Storage as a public profile image; anyone with its URL can view it.
- **Cloud AI** — Gemini-powered features send the requested prompt and, for speaking evaluation, recorded audio to Google Gemini.
- **Protected AI access** — cloud AI requires a signed-in account; the Gemini credential stays on Twino's server and is not included in the app.

## Data on your device

We store locally: lesson progress, XP, daily goal progress, streak, app language, Kurdish font choice, optional AI Teacher history, and preferences. Native authentication tokens use protected device credential storage.

If you create an account, Twino uses Supabase to store your email address, profile, preferences, avatar choice, and learning progress so they can sync across sessions. Core lessons remain usable without an account.

If you choose a photo while signed in, Twino uploads it to Supabase Storage as your public profile image. Anyone with its public URL can view it. You can use a bundled avatar instead. Replacing the photo overwrites the prior upload, and deleting your account removes the uploaded photo. A guest photo remains only on that device.

## Microphone & speech

Voice features use the device microphone and may use device speech recognition or cloud AI. When you choose a Gemini-powered speech feature, recorded audio, the requested phrase, and its related prompt are sent to Google Gemini for processing and learning feedback.

Typed answers, transcripts, and prompts may also be sent to Google Gemini when you choose an AI feature. Twino does not sell this data or use it for advertising.

## Your choices

You can deny microphone access and continue using non-voice lessons. Delete your account and synced data from **Settings → Your Account → Delete Account**. You can clear remaining local data through system settings or uninstall the app. If the in-app deletion option is unavailable, email **support@twino.app** from the account email address.
