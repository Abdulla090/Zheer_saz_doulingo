## Intent

Create an ultra-polished SaaS launch film for TWINO: sleek, modern, cinematic,
and unmistakably product-led. The film should make TWINO feel like the premium
daily system for learning English—not a generic lesson app.

## Assets

- The existing TWINO Expo project — use real product UI, mascot artwork, icons,
  and brand assets when they strengthen proof and credibility.

## Customizations

- Use premium UI choreography, spatial depth, kinetic typography, data motion,
  and seamless scene-to-scene handoffs.
- Generate supporting SaaS visuals when the existing UI does not supply the
  right cinematic composition.

## Notes

- Target duration is 45 seconds within the requested 30–60 second range.
- Landscape 16:9 is selected for a flagship YouTube/website launch film.
- Music-led with designed on-screen copy and no voice-over.
- Avoid generic purple-gradient SaaS clichés, repetitive card grids, and
  template-like motion.
