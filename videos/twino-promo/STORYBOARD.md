## Video direction

- Palette system: white canvas and near-black display type from `frame.md`; generated interface chrome uses TWINO yellow as the single accent. Native coral/blue may appear only inside the current app icon and approved character/feature artwork.
- Type and surface: DIN Next Rounded throughout; oversized editorial headlines, soft yellow-tinted surfaces, thin yellow borders, pill chrome, and no content shadows. Reconstructed product UI must feel like a cinematic native app, never a browser dashboard.
- Motion grammar: music-paced sequential reveals on smooth long-tail settles (`power3` default); each frame opens with one focal idea and releases further detail across the back half. Camera motion is finite and purposeful; after the payoff, it stops.
- Rhythm: Frames 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 build actively. Frame 2 is the identity breather and Frame 7 is the closing held read. During holds, use stillness or a finite low-amplitude jitter only—never breathing loops.
- Composition: every shot fills the top ~83% of the 1920×1080 canvas with one dominant focal at least 40% of the content region, asymmetric hierarchy, and 3 depth layers. The bottom caption keep-out remains clear even though captions are disabled.
- Negative list: no old app screenshots; no generic purple/blue AI gradients; no fake metrics; no browser chrome; no repetitive card grids; no drop-shadow stacks; no floating decorative bokeh; no slideshow front-load-and-freeze; no screensaver-style independent floating; no bounce-first motion; no infinite animation.
